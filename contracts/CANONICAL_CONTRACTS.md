# Genesis canonical contracts

The production boundary contains ten canonical modules, including `AppTeamDAO`:

- GenesisToken
- GenesisIssuance
- WorkerSplit
- GenesisEscrow
- GenesisOracle
- GenesisIdentityRegistry
- GenesisValidatorRegistry
- GenesisNoteRegistry
- GenesisGovernance
- AppTeamDAO

`AppTeamDAO` receives the 30% WorkerSplit App Team allocation and governs technical delivery, physical and digital integrations, release audits, issue incentives, and virtual-world partnerships. Changes to canonical allocation percentages or other protocol functions require a Genesis Improvement Proposal.
